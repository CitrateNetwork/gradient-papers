# ATIS: Let Cheap Analog Whisper "Skip This" Before the Expensive Math Runs — Comic / Deck Script

**LOGLINE:** Let cheap analog electronics whisper "skip this" before the expensive math runs.
**THE ONE IDEA:** Pre-filter the unimportant words (tokens) with tiny, low-power analog circuits, before the expensive digital attention math runs, to save energy.
**PANELS:** 14 · **PALETTE ACCENT:** cyan · **GUIDE-MOTIF:** Pip (a single warm-amber node of light)
**MATURITY:** Theoretical — a hardware research proposal at phase one. No prototype has been built. The honesty beat is central to this story, not a footnote.

> Art notes: every ART PROMPT ends with the shared STYLE TOKEN + NEGATIVE TOKEN from the Series Bible §8.
> Pip is described identically each time so it stays on-model. No text baked into art.
> Signature motif for this paper: **a sieve of light that drops dim motes and lets only bright ones
> pass through to a gate.** Visual grammar: **analog = warm flowing waves and gradients; digital =
> crisp cyan grid.** Honesty is the spine here, so panels 2 and 11 both carry it.

- **Runtime as a deck:** at ~8–10 seconds/panel this reads as a ~2:00 auto-advancing picture-book; pair with the screenplay VO for a fuller cut.
- **Reflow to 9:16:** stack caption under art; keep Pip centered; panels 6, 10, 11 are the beats to give the most motion if animated (the turn, the payoff, the catch).
- **Consistency:** reuse the exact Pip phrase ("a single small warm-amber node of light, soft bloom, no face") in every prompt so the character never drifts. Keep the warm-flow (analog) vs cool-cyan-grid (digital) grammar consistent across the whole piece.
- **Honesty is the spine, not a footnote:** panels 2 and 11 both carry it. Never cut them. The maturity label appears on the cover and back cover.
- **Facts check:** "60–80% of tokens skipped," the "coarse sieve before fine mill," "physics adds currents for free" (Kirchhoff), the binary keep/drop comparator (no ADC), and the DAC toll-booth bottleneck all trace to the ATIS paper (Sections 3, 4.2). Do not add numbers the paper doesn't support. NAT (Paper XI) explicitly does NOT implement ATIS — panel 13 keeps them apart on purpose.
