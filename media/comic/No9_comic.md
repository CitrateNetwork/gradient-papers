# The Medusa Paradigm: The Jellyfish Solved It First — Comic / Deck Script

**LOGLINE:** 500-million-year-old jellyfish already solved decentralized coordination.
**THE ONE IDEA:** Biology as design inspiration (analogy, not proof) for a leaderless network — a jellyfish's nerve net has no brain, yet coordinates the whole animal.
**PANELS:** 14 · **PALETTE ACCENT:** teal / magenta · **GUIDE-MOTIF:** Pip (a single warm-amber node of light)

> Art notes: every ART PROMPT ends with the shared STYLE TOKEN + NEGATIVE TOKEN from the Series Bible §8.
> Pip is described identically each time so it stays on-model. No text baked into art. This is the series'
> most poetic entry — lean into awe (ancient ocean, bioluminescence) while keeping the honesty crisp.
> KEEP all jellyfish biology; it is the subject and it is brand-safe.

- **Runtime as a deck:** at ~8–10 seconds/panel this reads as a ~2:00 auto-advancing picture-book; pair with the screenplay VO for a fuller cut.
- **Reflow to 9:16:** stack caption under art; keep Pip centered; panels 1, 5, 7, 13 are the "wow" beats — give them the most motion if animated. The nerve-net-dissolving-into-lattice (P5) is the signature image of this entry; spend budget there.
- **Consistency:** reuse the exact Pip phrase ("a single small warm-amber node of light, soft bloom, no face") in every prompt so the character never drifts.
- **Honesty beat is load-bearing:** Panel 11 (analogy-not-proof) and Panel 12 (guarantees come from math, not biology) are the whole paper's discipline. Never cut them; they are why the awe is earned.
- **Facts check:** all jellyfish biology (nerve net, *Aurelia*, *Turritopsis*, box-jelly eyes, nematocyst) traces to Paper IX and its cited literature. Keep the biology; do not invent numbers, and do not claim any biological mechanism runs a protocol.
