# The BR1J Constitution: The Rulebook a Network Writes for Itself — Comic / Deck Script

**LOGLINE:** The rulebook a self-governing network writes for itself, and can rewrite.
**THE ONE IDEA:** On-chain governance where your vote comes from your track record of contribution, not just the tokens you hold, with a living amendment process so the community can change any rule.
**PANELS:** 15 · **PALETTE ACCENT:** cyan/gold · **GUIDE-MOTIF:** Pip (a single warm-amber node of light)

> Art notes: every ART PROMPT ends with the shared STYLE TOKEN + NEGATIVE TOKEN from the Series Bible §8.
> Pip is described identically each time so it stays on-model. No text baked into art. Signature motif for
> this paper: a lattice that *votes and reshapes itself* (a parliament of light), and a save-point ring that
> can be *re-cast* (amended). This piece is legal-adjacent but not legal advice.
