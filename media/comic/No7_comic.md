# The Mozi Cooperative: The People Who Build the Models Should Own Them — Comic / Deck Script

**LOGLINE:** The people who train and support the models should own them.
**THE ONE IDEA:** Pay and grant ownership by measured contribution — running nodes, providing data, making adapters — not by who has the most capital.
**PANELS:** 14 · **PALETTE ACCENT:** gold · **GUIDE-MOTIF:** Pip (a single warm-amber node of light)

> Art notes: every ART PROMPT ends with the shared STYLE TOKEN + NEGATIVE TOKEN from the Series Bible §8.
> Pip is described identically each time so it stays on-model. No text baked into art. Signature motif for
> this paper: many small dots feeding one shared lattice, and light flowing *back* to each dot in proportion
> to what it gave. Value is always light, never coins.

## Producer notes
- **Runtime as a deck:** at ~8–10 seconds/panel this reads as a ~2:00 auto-advancing picture-book; pair with the screenplay VO for a fuller cut.
- **Signature motif discipline:** the "many dots feeding one lattice, light flowing back in proportion" appears in panels 1, 7, and 13 — keep the return-threads visibly *sized to* each dot's trail so the fairness reads without text.
- **Honesty beats are two panels (11 + 12)** and both are required: 11 = accounting-live-but-suite-not-yet-on-chain; 12 = free-riders / Sybil / slow governance named plainly. Never cut either.
- **Analogy for the illustrator:** think farmers' co-op, credit union, a writers' room sharing royalties, session musicians finally getting points on the record — belonging and fair shares, warm not corporate.
- **Facts check:** "accounting + reward is deployed" and "cooperative suite written but not yet on the main chain" trace to Paper VII §5–§6. The named unsolved problems (free-riders, Sybil, governance paralysis) trace to §6. No numbers, no earnings promises, no coins.
