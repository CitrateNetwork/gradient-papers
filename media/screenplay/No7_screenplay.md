# The Mozi Cooperative: The People Who Build the Models Should Own Them — Screenplay

**LOGLINE:** The people who train and support the models should own them.
**THE ONE IDEA:** Pay and grant ownership by measured contribution — running nodes, providing data, making adapters — not by who has the most capital.
**RUNTIME:** ~4:05 · **PALETTE ACCENT:** gold · **GUIDE-MOTIF:** Pip (a single warm-amber node of light, silent, reactive)
**COLD-OPEN LINE:** "A model is built by many hands. So who gets to own it?"

> Voice: neutral warm VO; Pip reacts, never speaks. Every ON SCREEN direction is written as motion so an
> animator or video model has clear verbs. Motifs reference the Series Bible §4 dictionary. Signature motif:
> many dots feeding one shared lattice, and value-light flowing back to each in proportion. Value is light,
> never coins. 16:9; 9:16 reflow notes where the composition changes.

---

### SCENE 1 — [0:00–0:30] — "Who Gets to Own It?"  (HOOK)
MOTIF: substrate; Pip; many contributor dots
ON SCREEN: black. Pip sparks into being and drifts forward; a deep-indigo lattice unfurls, and across it many faint dots do quiet work — one steadies a structure, one feeds in a stream of light-data, one shapes a small glowing graft of skill.
PIP: sparks, drifts, brightens as the many workers reveal — curious.
VO: "A model is built by many hands. People who run the machines. People who share their data. People who teach it new skills, one small lesson at a time. So here's a simple question. When that model becomes valuable, who gets to own it?"
SFX/MUSIC: low warm drone; a soft chime as the workers light up one by one.
SHOT NOTES: slow push-in on Pip; the many workers fade up around it. (9:16: workers stack vertically, Pip centered.)

### SCENE 2 — [0:30–1:00] — "The Light Pools at the Top"  (PROBLEM)
MOTIF: aggregation (the bad kind); the-bad-thing (concentration)
ON SCREEN: every worker's thread of value-light rushes upward and pools into one large distant node; the many dots below fade to dim grey. Then that lone top node shows a hairline crack, and the lattice beneath it trembles.
PIP: dims with unease; a small worried flicker.
VO: "Today, the answer is mostly about money. Whoever funded the compute owns the result, and the value pools at the top, while the people who did the work fade into the background. It isn't just unfair. It's fragile. When everything leans on a few, one bad day for them shakes the whole thing."
SFX/MUSIC: a low draining hum; music holds back, uneasy.
SHOT NOTES: the upward drain is the felt image; the crack lands on "fragile." (9:16: drain runs bottom-to-top.)

### SCENE 3 — [1:00–1:40] — "Measure the Work"  (THE IDEA)
MOTIF: node/Pip (understanding pulse); provenance trace; token/SALT
ON SCREEN: Pip pulses once. From each contributor dot a clean comet-trail of light is drawn, recording exactly what it did. Then the central lattice earns and brightens, and value-light streams back out to each dot, every return-thread sized to that dot's trail.
PIP: surges bright, pulses once — the "understanding" beat — then watches the light flow back.
VO: "The Mozi Cooperative starts from the opposite idea. Instead of asking who paid the most, it measures what each person actually contributed, and lets that decide their share. Every kind of help is counted. And when the model earns, the value flows back to each contributor, sized to what they really gave."
SFX/MUSIC: the hum releases into a warm rising swell; a soft chime on the pulse.
SHOT NOTES: the return-threads must visibly differ in thickness by contribution — that's the whole idea. (9:16: trails radiate from center.)

### SCENE 4 — [1:40–2:15] — "Every Kind of Help Counts"  (HOW IT WORKS 1)
MOTIF: named contributions; LoRA adapter / mentorship (graft handoff)
ON SCREEN: a row of distinct dots, each a different warm shade, does a different job — keeping blocks, hosting a bright core, pouring in data, raising an app-glyph. Then a bright dot grows a small graft and hands it to a dimmer dot, which brightens; as it does, the giver's own return-light grows too.
PIP: hops along the row, connecting each worker to the shared center.
VO: "Running a node, hosting a model, making an adapter, giving good data, building an app. All of it counts, not just the miners. And here's the quiet magic: when you share a skill that makes someone else better, the whole network is worth more, so helping others pays you back too. It's positive-sum."
SFX/MUSIC: light plucks as each worker lights; a warm swell on the graft handoff.
SHOT NOTES: the graft handoff (from Paper III's motif) is the hero beat here — both dots must rise together.

### SCENE 5 — [2:15–2:45] — "A Voice Sized to Your Work"  (HOW IT WORKS 2)
MOTIF: consensus/agreement; contribution-weighted governance
ON SCREEN: the dots pulse to vote; each vote-pulse is sized to its recorded trail. A dot that has worked honestly for a long time pulses large; a dot that just arrived pulses small. They align to one shared ring.
PIP: pulses in time with the vote, a quiet nod.
VO: "It also changes who gets a say. Your voice is weighted by your track record, so someone who has kept the network running honestly for a year outweighs someone who just showed up. Not the loudest wallet. The longest, steadiest hands."
SFX/MUSIC: soft layered tones resolving into one shared chord.
SHOT NOTES: size-of-pulse equals weight-of-vote; make the difference legible at a glance.

### SCENE 6 — [2:45–3:15] — "The Part That's Real"  (HOW IT WORKS 3 / GROUNDING)
MOTIF: proof/verification cyan; provenance trace
ON SCREEN: the central ledger-lattice crystallizes into a clean, checkable cyan structure holding every contributor's recorded trail; a cyan checkmark blooms. It reads as public and tamper-proof.
PIP: brightens as the checkmark blooms.
VO: "And this isn't only a sketch. The core that records contributions and pays out rewards is already built and live, a public ledger anyone can check, where the math decides the payout and no middleman is needed. That part is real, today."
SFX/MUSIC: a crystalline 'verify' chime; warm shimmer.
SHOT NOTES: reuse the series' verify-chime sound stamp. (9:16: ledger crystal center-frame.)

### SCENE 7 — [3:15–3:50] — "The Honest Catch"  (THE CATCH)
MOTIF: honesty beat (Pip dims, "?"); danger red (light touch)
ON SCREEN: the scene cools; Pip dims and a small "?" of light hovers. One solid cyan crystal sits finished ("live"); beside it, a half-drawn gold lattice hangs just off the main structure, unfinished. Then three faint warning-motes drift up: a dot faking many copies of itself, a dim dot coasting on others' light, a ring turning very slowly.
PIP: dims — the honest, quiet beat of the whole series.
VO: "Now the honest part. The accounting is live, but the fuller cooperative, the shared model ownership, the membership, is written in code and not yet running on the main network. We'll say so plainly. And cooperatives have old, unsolved problems: people faking many identities to grab extra credit, free-riders coasting on others' work, and group decisions that move slowly. We don't claim to have solved any of them."
SFX/MUSIC: music thins to a single sustained note; space to breathe.
SHOT NOTES: lower brightness overall; sincere, not scary. The three warning-motes are a light touch of red, quickly and calmly.

### SCENE 8 — [3:50–4:20] — "Why It Matters, and What's Next"  (WHY + TIE + BUTTON)
MOTIF: token/SALT; consensus in concert; series tie; Pip travels
ON SCREEN: pull back — the whole cooperative lattice glows in balance: many dots giving, one shared model earning, value-light flowing back to each in fair proportion. Then Pip zips down an edge toward a faint region ahead where a lattice reshapes and re-votes itself.
PIP: settles, glows steadily among equals, then darts off toward Paper VIII.
VO: "Picture a farmers' co-op, or a writers' room where everyone shares the royalties, or session musicians finally getting points on the record. That's the aim here: infrastructure owned by the people who build it, where the value flows back to the many instead of up to the few. Next, we ask how a network like this writes, and rewrites, its own rulebook."
SFX/MUSIC: full warm resolve; a single bright rising note as Pip departs.
SHOT NOTES: end card is light-only: the balanced lattice, evenly lit. Title fades: "The Gradient Papers · No. VII". (9:16: Pip exits upward.)

## Producer notes
- **Total VO:** ~515 words ≈ 4:00–4:15 at a warm ~125 wpm. Trim one sentence from Scene 4 or Scene 5 for a tight 3:40.
- **Hero beats:** Scene 3 (measure-the-work + light flowing back, the signature motif) and Scene 4 (the graft handoff / positive-sum) are the two moments to spend animation budget on.
- **Honesty beat is non-negotiable** (Scene 7) and carries a double load this paper: (1) accounting live but co-op suite not yet on the main chain; (2) free-riders, Sybil, and slow governance named plainly. Never cut it.
- **Signature motif discipline:** keep the return-value threads visibly *sized to* each contributor's trail in Scenes 3 and 8, so "fair share" reads without any text.
- **Music:** reuse the series' warm theme and the 'verify chime' (S6) and 'rising note' (S8/departure) sound stamps.
- **Fact anchors:** deployed contribution-and-reward accounting vs. specified-but-not-deployed cooperative suite (Paper VII §5); the unsolved-problems list (§6); contribution-weighted governance and positive-sum knowledge-sharing (§2–§3). No figures the paper doesn't support, no coins, no earnings promises.
