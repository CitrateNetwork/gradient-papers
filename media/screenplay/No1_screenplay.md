# Citrate: The Public Computer That Runs AI and Can Prove It — Screenplay

**LOGLINE:** A public computer many strangers run together, that also runs AI and can prove it played fair.
**THE ONE IDEA:** Consensus and computation, done in parallel, with AI built in and checkable.
**RUNTIME:** ~4:10 · **PALETTE ACCENT:** gold · **GUIDE-MOTIF:** Pip (a single warm-amber node of light, silent, reactive)
**COLD-OPEN LINE:** "Imagine a computer that belongs to no one — and to everyone."

> Voice: neutral warm VO; Pip reacts, never speaks. Every ON SCREEN direction is written as motion so
> an animator or video model has clear verbs. Motifs reference the Series Bible §4 dictionary. 16:9;
> 9:16 reflow notes where the composition changes.

---

### SCENE 1 — [0:00–0:28] — "A Computer That Belongs to Everyone"  (HOOK)
MOTIF: substrate; Pip
ON SCREEN: black. A single warm-amber node (Pip) sparks into being and drifts forward; as it moves, a vast thin-line indigo lattice unfurls around it in every direction, revealing scale.
PIP: sparks, drifts, brightens as the world reveals — curious.
VO: "Imagine a computer that belongs to no one, and to everyone. Thousands of strangers run it together. No company owns it. And we wanted it to do something new — not just move money, but run AI."
SFX/MUSIC: low warm drone; a soft chime as the lattice unfurls.
SHOT NOTES: slow push-in on Pip; lattice parallax reveal. (9:16: keep Pip centered, unfurl vertically.)

### SCENE 2 — [0:28–0:55] — "The Old Way Was a Single Line"  (HOOK→PROBLEM)
MOTIF: the-bad-thing (single-file); block crystals
ON SCREEN: gold block-crystals are forced into one narrow lane, moving one at a time down a slow line; a long tail of crystals waits. Pip dims with impatience.
PIP: dims slightly; a small impatient flicker.
VO: "But most blockchains have a bottleneck. Everything waits in a single line — one block at a time. Safe, but slow. Too slow for a machine that has to think."
SFX/MUSIC: a metronomic tick; music holds back.
SHOT NOTES: side-on, emphasize the length of the waiting tail. (9:16: line runs top-to-bottom.)

### SCENE 3 — [0:55–1:30] — "Open Every Lane"  (THE IDEA)
MOTIF: GhostDAG parallel crystals → spine
ON SCREEN: the single lane bursts open into many parallel lanes; gold crystals form simultaneously across all of them — then thread together into one clean glowing spine. Pip surges bright.
PIP: surges bright, spins once — the "understanding" pulse.
VO: "Citrate's answer is to open every lane at once. Many blocks form at the same time, in parallel — and then they weave back into one shared order everyone agrees on. Fast, and still one story."
SFX/MUSIC: the tick releases into a rising swell; whoosh on the burst.
SHOT NOTES: the burst is a hero moment — wide, kinetic; settle on the threading spine. (9:16: burst radiates from center.)

### SCENE 4 — [1:30–1:58] — "A Save Point Nobody Can Undo"  (HOW IT WORKS 1)
MOTIF: checkpoint (pulsing ring, finality)
ON SCREEN: a bright white ring pulses around a section of the spine and locks it into solid, unbreakable crystal. A faint tally shows most-of-the-network agreeing before the lock snaps.
PIP: pulses once in time with the ring — a nod.
VO: "Every so often, a committee of the network signs off and locks a section in place — a save point that can't be rewound. Once it's set, it's final."
SFX/MUSIC: a deep, satisfying lock-thunk; warm sustain.
SHOT NOTES: macro on the ring closing; the locked crystal gets a subtle hardened texture.

### SCENE 5 — [1:58–2:32] — "It Runs Today's Apps — and Runs AI"  (HOW IT WORKS 2)
MOTIF: compatibility glyphs; AI slots (cyan); determinism grid
ON SCREEN: abstract app-glyphs (not logos) slot into the lattice and light up. Then camera finds special cyan-edged slots; an AI computation runs inside one, and a snap-to-grid ripple spreads so every node shows the identical result.
PIP: hops between an app-glyph and a cyan slot, connecting them.
VO: "It speaks the common language of smart contracts, so today's tools just work. But it also has AI built into the machine itself — and here's the trick: those AI steps run exactly the same on every computer, down to the last digit."
SFX/MUSIC: light plucks as glyphs slot; a clean tonal 'set' when the ripple aligns.
SHOT NOTES: the identical ripple across many nodes sells 'determinism' — synchronize it precisely.

### SCENE 6 — [2:32–3:05] — "Check the AI's Homework"  (HOW IT WORKS 3)
MOTIF: proof (crystallizing gem + cyan check); token/SALT
ON SCREEN: a messy tangle of light — an AI's work — crystallizes into a clean cyan gem; a cyan checkmark blooms. Then warm-amber motes flow from the working parts back to the nodes that did the work.
PIP: brightens as the checkmark blooms; catches a mote of value-light.
VO: "And it can check the AI's homework — a small proof that the work was really done right, without anyone having to redo it. The reward for doing that work is a fuel called SALT, and it flows to the people who actually run and improve the network."
SFX/MUSIC: a crystalline 'verify' chime; warm shimmer as value flows.
SHOT NOTES: value-light is motion of light, never coins. (9:16: gem center, motes rain down.)

### SCENE 7 — [3:05–3:40] — "The Honest Catch"  (THE CATCH)
MOTIF: honesty beat (Pip dims, "?")
ON SCREEN: the scene cools; Pip dims and a small "?" of light hovers. Two soft light-plates flicker: one crystal half-formed ("a goal, not yet proven"), one cyan gate shut ("off by default").
PIP: dims; the honest, quiet beat of the whole series.
VO: "We try to stay honest about what's finished. Our headline speed is a goal we're building toward, not a finished benchmark. And the strongest proof mode is real, but it ships switched off by default for now. We'll say so, every time."
SFX/MUSIC: music thins to a single sustained note; space to breathe.
SHOT NOTES: lower brightness overall; this beat must feel sincere, not scary.

### SCENE 8 — [3:40–4:10] — "Why It Matters, and What's Next"  (WHY + TIE + BUTTON)
MOTIF: consensus in concert; series tie; Pip travels
ON SCREEN: pull back — the whole lattice glows in concert: parallel crystals, save-point rings, cyan proofs, gold value flowing. Then Pip zips down an edge toward faint colored regions ahead (a magenta split node glints).
PIP: settles, glows steadily, then darts off toward Paper II.
VO: "Put it together and you get a public place to run AI where you don't have to trust the operator — you can check them. That's the ground floor. Next, we ask a stranger question: what if a network treated disagreement not as noise, but as information?"
SFX/MUSIC: full warm resolve; a single bright rising note as Pip departs.
SHOT NOTES: end card is light-only (no logo lockup needed): the steady node on the spine. Title fades: "The Gradient Papers · No. I". (9:16: Pip exits upward.)

## Producer notes
- **Total VO:** ~520 words ≈ 4:00–4:15 at a warm ~125 wpm. Trim Scene 5 or 6 by one sentence for a tight 3:30.
- **Hero beats:** Scene 3 (parallel burst) and Scene 6 (proof + value) are the two moments to spend animation budget on.
- **Honesty beat is non-negotiable** (Scene 7): it's the series' signature and its credibility. Never cut it to save time.
- **Music:** one evolving warm theme; the "lock-thunk" (S4), "verify chime" (S6), and "rising note" (S8/departure) are the three signature sound stamps reused across the whole series.
- **Fact anchors:** save-point quorum, deterministic AI math, verifiable-inference proof, SALT-to-contributors, and both honesty caveats all trace to Paper I (`Gradient_Papers_No1_Citrate_Technical_Paper_v3.md`). Do not introduce figures the paper doesn't support.
