# The Mentorship Protocol: Teach the Network Like a Good Mentor — Screenplay

**LOGLINE:** Teach the network like a good mentor teaches a student.
**THE ONE IDEA:** Strong nodes hand small 'grafts' of skill to weaker ones at save-points, and the network improves as a team.
**RUNTIME:** ~4:10 · **PALETTE ACCENT:** gold · **GUIDE-MOTIF:** Pip (a single warm-amber node of light, silent, reactive)
**COLD-OPEN LINE:** "The best way to get better isn't to be averaged with everyone else. It's to be taught."

> Voice: neutral warm VO; Pip reacts, never speaks. Every ON SCREEN direction is written as motion so
> an animator or video model has clear verbs. Motifs reference the Series Bible §4 dictionary. 16:9;
> 9:16 reflow notes where the composition changes. Signature motion this issue: the **graft handoff**.

---

### SCENE 1 — [0:00–0:30] — "A Network That Just Averages"  (HOOK)
MOTIF: node/Pip; the-bad-thing (averaging to grey)
ON SCREEN: many bright and dim nodes pour their light into one hub; the hub blends it into a flat grey and pushes an identical grey copy back to each. Pip watches and dims.
PIP: drifts in curious, then dims with concern as the color drains to grey.
VO: "The best way to get better isn't to be averaged with everyone else. It's to be taught. But most shared-learning networks just take everyone's work and average it. The mix goes grey. The strong get pulled down, the weak barely rise, and nobody is actually taught anything."
SFX/MUSIC: a flat, colorless hum; music holds back.
SHOT NOTES: emphasize color draining to grey as the hub blends. (9:16: hub centered, nodes ring it.)

### SCENE 2 — [0:30–1:12] — "Hand Over a Graft"  (THE IDEA)
MOTIF: graft handoff (mentorship) — signature motif
ON SCREEN: contrast wipe to warm color. A bright node notices one dim patch in a neighbor, grows a small glowing gold graft aimed at exactly that patch, and hands it across an edge. The dimmer node brightens as it receives it. Pip surges bright.
PIP: leans in, then surges bright and spins once — the "understanding" pulse.
VO: "A good mentor does the opposite. They spot your one weak spot and hand you one targeted drill. So this network does that too. A strong node grows a small graft of skill, aimed at exactly the thing a weaker node is missing, and hands it over. And the weaker node lights up. That's the whole idea, teach the parts that are behind, instead of blurring everyone into the middle."
SFX/MUSIC: the hum releases into a warm rising swell; a soft "brighten" chime on the handoff.
SHOT NOTES: the handoff is the hero beat — land the brighten clearly. (9:16: mentor above, mentee below.)

### SCENE 3 — [1:12–1:42] — "Only Trusted Nodes Teach"  (HOW IT WORKS 1)
MOTIF: node (brightness = trust); attestation gate (cyan)
ON SCREEN: candidate mentors pass through a cyan gate that checks their track record; only the brightest, well-proven nodes are stamped genuine and allowed through to teach; dimmer or off-color ones are held back.
PIP: hovers by the gate, nodding as each proven node passes.
VO: "Not just anyone can mentor. A node has to have earned it, with a long, honest track record the whole network can see. Only proven nodes are allowed to teach. That keeps a careless or dishonest node from handing out bad advice."
SFX/MUSIC: soft tonal 'pass' chime as each mentor clears the gate.
SHOT NOTES: the gate scan reads as a credential check, calm not harsh.

### SCENE 4 — [1:42–2:22] — "The Central Oracle Is a Save-Point"  (HOW IT WORKS 2)
MOTIF: checkpoint (pulsing ring); honesty beat (rounds, not moments)
ON SCREEN: a pulsing gold-white ring closes around a section of lattice, the Central Oracle. Inside it, mentors and mentees are matched by who complements whom, and the round locks in. Then Pip dims briefly and a small "?" hovers as the timing is clarified.
PIP: pulses once with the ring, then dims for the honest aside.
VO: "The matchmaker is a pulsing ring the paper calls the Central Oracle. It's really a save-point, the network gathers, pairs each mentor with a mentee who needs them, and locks the lesson in. One honest detail, though: this happens in rounds, not every instant. The network improves cycle by cycle, a step at a time, not continuously."
SFX/MUSIC: a warm lock-thunk as the ring closes; music thins for the aside.
SHOT NOTES: the honest aside gets lower brightness; sincere, not alarming.

### SCENE 5 — [2:22–2:54] — "Guard Against a Bad Mentor"  (HOW IT WORKS 3)
MOTIF: proof/verify (cyan); slashing/defense (red)
ON SCREEN: a graft is replayed and confirmed by a blooming cyan checkmark before it sticks. A separate off-color node tries to pass a poisoned graft; an automatic red sting fires and its graft is pulled back and dissolved.
PIP: brightens at the cyan check; flinches, then steadies as the red sting handles the bad actor.
VO: "There's a real risk here, a bad mentor could hand over a graft that quietly makes a student worse. So every graft can be checked by replaying it, and undone if it doesn't hold up. A mentor whose lessons make students worse gets those lessons revoked."
SFX/MUSIC: a crystalline 'verify' chime; a short sharp sting on the reject.
SHOT NOTES: keep the red beat brief; the tone is protective, not scary.

### SCENE 6 — [2:54–3:18] — "Today's Student, Tomorrow's Teacher"  (HOW IT WORKS 4)
MOTIF: graft handoff (mobility); routing (specialization)
ON SCREEN: the once-dim mentee, now bright, grows its own graft and hands it onward. Nearby, question-motes fly to whichever node glows brightest for their kind of question, so distinct specialists form across the lattice.
PIP: darts between the new teacher and a forming specialist, delighted.
VO: "And it doesn't stop. A student who improves can turn around and become a teacher. Over many rounds, questions flow to whichever node is best at them, so nodes specialize, each becoming the expert at its own thing, and the network fills with complementary specialists instead of identical copies."
SFX/MUSIC: light plucks as specialists light up in their own hues.
SHOT NOTES: let a few distinct hues emerge to sell specialization.

### SCENE 7 — [3:18–3:52] — "The Honest Catch"  (THE CATCH)
MOTIF: honesty beat (Pip dims, "?")
ON SCREEN: the scene cools; Pip dims and a small "?" of light hovers. Two soft light-plates flicker: one a steady working gear ("built and running on-chain"), one a half-formed result ("not yet measured").
PIP: dims; the quiet, honest beat of the whole series.
VO: "Here's what we can and can't claim. The mentoring machine is built, and it runs on-chain, the pairing, the grafts, the registry, all real. But whether it actually makes the team learn faster, and grow into complementary experts, is still an untested idea. We've designed the experiment. We don't have the result yet, and we won't pretend we do."
SFX/MUSIC: music thins to a single sustained note; space to breathe.
SHOT NOTES: lower overall brightness; this beat must feel sincere.

### SCENE 8 — [3:52–4:12] — "Why It Matters, and What's Next"  (WHY + TIE + BUTTON)
MOTIF: team learning; series tie; Pip travels
ON SCREEN: pull back, grafts pass hand to hand across the lattice, dim nodes brighten, specialists glow in their own colors, the network lifting together. Then Pip zips down an edge toward faint regions ahead, a cluster of dots feeding light back to each other.
PIP: settles, glows steadily, then darts off toward the next paper.
VO: "Put it together and you get a network that learns like a team, teaching its weakest parts instead of averaging them away. It's built on the disagreement-keeping network from Paper Two. Next, we ask who should own the models they help train."
SFX/MUSIC: full warm resolve; a single bright rising note as Pip departs.
SHOT NOTES: end card is light-only: the steady node beside one it just brightened. Title fades: "The Gradient Papers · No. III". (9:16: Pip exits upward.)

## Producer notes
- **Total VO:** ~510 words ≈ 4:00–4:15 at a warm ~125 wpm. Trim Scene 5 or 6 by one sentence for a tight 3:40.
- **Hero beats:** Scene 2 (the graft handoff) and Scene 6 (student-becomes-teacher + specialists) are the two moments to spend animation budget on. The graft handoff is this issue's signature motion — reuse it identically wherever grafts pass.
- **Honesty beats are non-negotiable:** Scene 4 (improvement happens in rounds/cycles, not per-moment) and Scene 7 (mechanism built and on-chain, but team-learning outcomes are an untested hypothesis with a designed experiment). Never cut either to save time; they are the series' credibility.
- **Music:** one evolving warm theme; the "brighten chime" (S2), "lock-thunk" (S4), and "verify chime + sting" (S5) are the signature sound stamps for this issue; keep the "rising note" (S8/departure) shared with the rest of the series.
- **Fact anchors:** mentor gating, the graft (LoRA-adapter) handoff, adapter verification/rollback, the checkpoint-as-Central-Oracle, and emergent specialization all trace to Paper III (built + on-chain). "Rounds not per-moment" traces to the paper's own honesty note on cycles vs. checkpoints; the untested-outcomes caveat traces to its Hypothesis sections. Do not introduce figures the paper doesn't support.
