# NAT: A Mind With Labeled Rooms — Screenplay

**LOGLINE:** A mind with labeled rooms, so you can always see which room did the thinking.
**THE ONE IDEA:** Split the model into declared, named zones over a fixed layout, so every answer comes with a replayable record of which zones did the work — verifiable by construction, and (in the tests so far) doing more with the same number of parameters at small scale.
**RUNTIME:** ~4:05 · **PALETTE ACCENT:** six-hue zone palette · **GUIDE-MOTIF:** Pip (a single warm-amber node of light, silent, reactive)
**COLD-OPEN LINE:** "Here is the strange thing about most AI: when it answers you, no one can point to the part that did the thinking."

> Voice: neutral warm VO; Pip reacts, never speaks. Every ON SCREEN direction is written as motion.
> Motifs reference the Series Bible §4 dictionary — named zones (zone palette), the provenance trace as a
> gold+cyan comet-trail, the honesty beat (Pip dims, "?"). 16:9; 9:16 reflow notes where composition changes.

### SCENE 1 — [0:00–0:32] — "The Blur"  (HOOK)
MOTIF: aggregation/blur (the bad thing); Pip curious
ON SCREEN: black. A single large blob of grey-gold light swells into view; a question-mote enters one side and an answer-mote leaves the other, but the middle stays an unreadable smear. Pip drifts up and tries to peer inside; the surface stays smooth and closed.
PIP: drifts in, tilts, tries to see in — and can't.
VO: "Here is the strange thing about most AI. When it answers you, no one can point to the part that did the thinking. Inside, it's one big undifferentiated blob of numbers. Every question goes through the whole thing, and the answer comes out the far side. It works. But it's a blur."
SFX/MUSIC: low warm drone; a muffled, closed sound behind the blob.
SHOT NOTES: slow push toward the opaque surface; keep the interior deliberately unreadable. (9:16: blob fills center, motes enter top and exit bottom.)

### SCENE 2 — [0:32–1:02] — "One Chef, No Receipt"  (PROBLEM)
MOTIF: the-bad-thing (opacity); stakes
ON SCREEN: the blob becomes a single frantic swirl of motion in one unlabeled space, flinging finished plates of light outward. A blank receipt-shape curls at the edge and stays empty. Three cool spotlights try to enter the blob and glance off.
PIP: dims a little, uneasy.
VO: "Picture one chef cooking everything in a single blur. The food arrives, but there's no receipt — no record of who made what, or how. For a casual chat, fine. But for a medical or legal decision you have to be able to replay, that blur is disqualifying. You can't prove what happened. You can only guess after the fact."
SFX/MUSIC: restless motion; a thin, searching tone under the spotlights.
SHOT NOTES: emphasize the blank receipt and the light glancing off — nothing gets in. (9:16: swirl center, spotlights rake across.)

### SCENE 3 — [1:02–1:38] — "Draw The Rooms First"  (THE IDEA)
MOTIF: named zones (NAT); Pip understanding-pulse
ON SCREEN: the blob cracks cleanly along declared lines and resolves into six labeled glowing rooms in six distinct palette hues, each pulsing on its own beat over a fixed floor plan. Five rooms glow; one small controller room stays dim and only sequences. Pip surges bright — the understanding pulse.
PIP: pulses once, bright — "aha."
VO: "NAT's move is simple to say. Instead of one blur, draw the rooms first. Split the model into named zones over a fixed layout, decided up front. One zone takes things in. One handles memory. The deepest one does the reasoning. A small controller only directs traffic — it never cooks. Now the floor plan is something you can read."
SFX/MUSIC: the drone resolves into a clean chord as the rooms snap into place.
SHOT NOTES: the crack-and-resolve is a hero beat — let each zone light on its own beat. (9:16: rooms stack; controller sits at the base.)

### SCENE 4 — [1:38–2:08] — "The Guide That Can't Cheat"  (HOW IT WORKS 1)
MOTIF: routing (question-mote to matching zone); determinism
ON SCREEN: a question-mote flies in and lights only the zones it needs, traveling along pre-drawn glowing doors between rooms. It brightens and dims those doors — but when it pushes toward a wall with no door, solid dark lattice blocks it; there is simply no path.
PIP: rides alongside the mote, testing the walls.
VO: "A learned guide sends each question to the rooms that fit it. But — and this is the point — it can only use doors that were drawn in advance. It can turn a path up or down, but it can never invent a new one. The wiring is fixed, declared, and checkable. The model stays adaptive without going off the map."
SFX/MUSIC: light plucks as doors brighten; a soft, firm thud where the mote meets a wall.
SHOT NOTES: sell the blocked direction clearly — no doorway, no glow. (9:16: mote travels top-to-bottom through the rooms.)

### SCENE 5 — [2:08–2:44] — "The Receipt Is The Product"  (HOW IT WORKS 2)
MOTIF: provenance trace ("the trace is the product"); proof cyan; on-chain commitment
ON SCREEN: as the answer forms, a gold-and-cyan comet-trail records the exact path — which rooms fired, in what order, with what weight — and crystallizes into a clean itemized receipt of light. The receipt stamps onto the lattice, and an independent second trace re-runs just that decision on a crisp integer grid, matching it exactly. A cyan checkmark blooms.
PIP: catches the comet-trail, brightens as the checkmark blooms.
VO: "And every answer comes with a receipt. A trace that records exactly which zones fired, in what order, with what weight. The receipt isn't a debugging afterthought — it's the product. Because the key decisions run on exact whole-number math, a stranger can replay that receipt and get the identical result. It's verifiable by construction, with no giant proof to grind out."
SFX/MUSIC: a crystalline "verify" chime as the receipt forms and the checkmark blooms.
SHOT NOTES: the comet-trail is the signature image of the whole piece — spend budget here. (9:16: trail runs vertically, receipt center.)

### SCENE 6 — [2:44–3:16] — "Does More With The Same Parts"  (HOW IT WORKS 3)
MOTIF: capability-per-parameter; named zones
ON SCREEN: two equally sized rigs sit side by side — a single grey blob and the six-zone build — fed the identical stream of amber fuel. The zoned build reaches a little higher and glows a touch brighter. Beside them, a small three-rung ladder shows the gap growing modestly at each rung.
PIP: hovers between the two rigs, noting the zoned one edging ahead.
VO: "Here's the part that surprised us. Give the zoned model and a plain blob the exact same number of parts and the same fuel, and the zoned one did a little more. And across the small sizes we tested, that edge didn't shrink — it grew, from a hair to a clear margin. The same skill, from fewer parts. At least at small scale, structure seems to earn its keep."
SFX/MUSIC: a quiet rising figure as the zoned rig edges ahead.
SHOT NOTES: keep both rigs visibly identical in size so the difference reads as structure, not scale. (9:16: rigs stack, ladder to the side.)

### SCENE 7 — [3:16–3:52] — "The Honest Catch"  (THE CATCH)
MOTIF: honesty beat (Pip dims, "?"); scale
ON SCREEN: the scene cools. Pip dims; a small "?" of light hovers. Three soft light-plates flicker in turn: a careful level bar labeled "at least as good," not a victory flag; a cluster of only five dots, all leaning the same way but few in number; then the small ladder continuing up until its widening gap flattens at a much taller rung, over which the "?" settles.
PIP: dims — the honest, quiet beat of the whole series.
VO: "Now the honest part. The claim is 'at least as good,' not 'beats.' It did come out better on all five test runs — but with only five, that's suggestive, not a formal proof. And the edge clearly widened only up to about two million parts. At a far larger run it went flat, and a change in precision muddied the reading. So beyond that small range, whether it keeps improving is simply unresolved. We'll say so."
SFX/MUSIC: music thins to a single sustained note; room to breathe.
SHOT NOTES: lower brightness overall; sincere, not scary. (9:16: plates stack vertically, "?" holds at top.)

### SCENE 8 — [3:52–4:05] — "Why It Matters, and What's Next"  (WHY + TIE + BUTTON)
MOTIF: provenance trace; named zones in concert; series tie; Pip travels
ON SCREEN: pull back — the six zones glow in concert, the comet-trail receipt threading cleanly through them, a cyan checkmark settling over the whole; a black box turned to glass. A fainter blueprint of many nodes each holding one zone flickers as "specified, not yet built." Then Pip zips down a lattice edge toward the next paper's region.
PIP: settles, glows steadily, then darts onward.
VO: "The real novelty isn't stitching old ideas together. It's the replayable receipt of exactly which room did the thinking — a black box turned to glass. Training this across a whole network is designed but not yet demonstrated. What's real today is the glass box itself. Declare the rooms, and interpretability, efficiency, and decentralization become three views of one decision."
SFX/MUSIC: full warm resolve; a single bright rising note as Pip departs.
SHOT NOTES: end card is light-only: the six steady zones and their receipt. Title fades: "The Gradient Papers · No. XI". (9:16: Pip exits upward.)

## Producer notes
- **Total VO:** ~515 words ≈ 4:00–4:10 at a warm ~125 wpm. Trim one sentence from Scene 2 or Scene 6 for a tight 3:45.
- **Hero beats:** Scene 3 (blob cracking into six rooms) and Scene 5 (the comet-trail receipt + independent replay) are the two moments to spend animation budget on.
- **Honesty beat is non-negotiable** (Scene 7): "at least as good," not "beats"; five seeds is suggestive not significant; widening scoped to ≤ ~2M parameters, flat and precision-confounded above. Never cut it to save time, and never upgrade the wording.
- **Music:** one evolving warm theme; the "verify chime" (S5) and "rising note" (S8/departure) are the shared sound stamps from the series.
- **Fact anchors:** named zones over fixed topology, the router that cannot create an undeclared edge, the decision-faithful replayable trace on the Q16.16 integer path, capability-per-parameter (0.024 → 0.141 bits/byte, 5/5 seeds, ≤ 2M parameters), the 32M flat/bf16-confounded rerun, and the federated layer being specified-not-demonstrated all trace to Paper XI (`Gradient_Papers_No11_NAT_v3.md`). Do not introduce figures the paper doesn't support.
