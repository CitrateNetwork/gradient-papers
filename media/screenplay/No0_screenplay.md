# The Gradient Papers: A Map of the Whole Set — Trailer Screenplay

**LOGLINE:** A 90-second map of the whole set.
**THE ONE IDEA:** Consensus and learning are the same process — a network that learns by agreeing.
**RUNTIME:** ~1:45 · **PALETTE ACCENT:** full palette · **GUIDE-MOTIF:** Pip (a single warm-amber node of light, silent, reactive)
**COLD-OPEN LINE:** "A blockchain agrees. A brain learns. What if those are the same thing?"

> This is the overview cut, not a full piece: a tight ~1:45 trailer. Cold open on the thesis, a fast
> flythrough where Pip lights each of the eleven regions in one line, one honest beat, then a button.
> Neutral warm VO; Pip reacts, never speaks. ON SCREEN written as motion verbs. Motifs from Bible §4.
> 16:9; 9:16 reflow notes where the composition changes. VO ≈ 280 words at a brisk ~160 wpm.

---

### SCENE 1 — [0:00–0:16] — "The Same Thing"  (COLD OPEN / THESIS)
MOTIF: substrate; consensus→agreement; determinism grid
ON SCREEN: black. Pip sparks into being. Scattered dots pulse and snap into one ring, and in the same motion light flows back out to each dot — agreement and learning as a single gesture. The lattice unfurls to reveal a vast map.
PIP: sparks, brightens, drifts to the center of the ring.
VO: "A blockchain agrees. A brain learns. What if those are the same thing? That's the whole idea behind the Gradient Papers: a network that learns by agreeing. Here's the map."
SFX/MUSIC: low warm drone; a single bright chime as the ring locks.
SHOT NOTES: slow push-in on Pip, then a fast pull-back to reveal eleven distant colored regions. (9:16: ring centered, regions stacked.)

### SCENE 2 — [0:16–1:05] — "The Flythrough"  (ELEVEN REGIONS, ONE LINE EACH)
MOTIF: full palette; Pip travels, lighting each zone in turn
ON SCREEN: Pip flies the lattice as one continuous shot, diving into each region and lighting it in its own color before darting to the next. Each region shows its signature motif for a beat.
PIP: travels fast and joyfully; takes on each region's tint, then moves on.
VO:
"One — a public computer that runs AI and can prove it. Built.
Two — it treats disagreement as information, not noise. Built.
Three — it teaches the network like a mentor. Built, with the results still being tested.
Four — guardrails so AI coders build what you asked, and nothing sneaky. A practice we live by.
Five — analog electronics that whisper 'skip this' before the costly math runs. Honestly, still just an idea.
Six — value leaves the network without selling the fuel that runs it. Built, as a wrapped token and a market-maker, not the bridge we first drew.
Seven — the people who train the models own them. The accounting is built.
Eight — the rulebook the network writes for itself, and can rewrite. Built.
Nine — five-hundred-million-year-old jellyfish already solved leaderless coordination. Inspiration, not proof.
Ten — checking an AI's homework without redoing it. Built, with one proof mode off by default.
Eleven — a mind with labeled rooms, so you see which one did the thinking. Built."
SFX/MUSIC: a rising rhythmic pulse, one soft stamp per region; tempo builds.
SHOT NOTES: keep it kinetic and unbroken; roughly four seconds per region. Region colors match each paper's spine accent. (9:16: Pip dives downward region to region.)

### SCENE 3 — [1:05–1:24] — "What's Real, What's Drawn"  (HONESTY BEAT)
MOTIF: honesty beat (Pip dims, "?"); real vs proposed
ON SCREEN: the map cools and holds still. Pip dims; a small "?" of light hovers. Across the eleven regions, some glow solid and some fade to faint blueprints — the difference visible on the map itself.
PIP: dims; the honest, quiet beat of the whole series.
VO: "You noticed we kept saying built, or idea, or inspiration. That's the point. This series always marks what's running today and what's only proposed. The map never pretends they're the same."
SFX/MUSIC: music thins to a single sustained note; space to breathe.
SHOT NOTES: lower brightness overall; sincere, not scary. Built regions stay solid, proposals read as outlines.

### SCENE 4 — [1:24–1:45] — "Start Anywhere"  (BUTTON / THESIS RESTATED)
MOTIF: consensus in concert; full palette; the one idea restated
ON SCREEN: warmth returns; every region glows in concert across the full palette, threaded by one spine. Pip rises to the center, steady and bright.
PIP: settles at the center, glowing steadily, then holds.
VO: "Eleven papers, one lattice that learns by agreeing. Consensus and learning, the same process. Start anywhere."
SFX/MUSIC: full warm resolve; a single bright rising note.
SHOT NOTES: end card is light-only: the steady node at the center of the lit lattice. Title fades: "The Gradient Papers · No. 0". (9:16: Pip holds center, regions ring around it.)

## Producer notes
- **Total VO:** ~280 words ≈ 1:45 at a brisk ~160 wpm. This is the trailer pace on purpose, faster than the 4-minute per-paper cut. Trim one or two region tags for a tight 1:30 short.
- **The flythrough is the hero (Scene 2):** one continuous camera, Pip lighting each zone in its own color. This is where the animation budget goes. Keep the beat metronomic so eleven regions don't blur.
- **Honesty beat is non-negotiable (Scene 3):** it's the series' signature and its credibility. The built / idea / inspiration / off-by-default tags are the papers' own status words (Series Index maturity tags); never upgrade one to sound further along.
- **Music stamps carry over from the series:** the "verify chime" and the "rising note" (departure) reused from the per-paper pieces tie this trailer to the set.
- **Fact anchors:** every maturity tag traces to the Series Index v3 status table (I Verified, II/III/VI/VII/VIII/X/XI Implemented, IV Practiced, V Theoretical, IX Specified+Practiced; VI's superseded NFT bridge; X's off-by-default verifier). Do not introduce figures the papers don't support.
