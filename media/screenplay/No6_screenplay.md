# The Memetic Money Portal: Letting Value Leave Without Selling the Fuel — Screenplay

**LOGLINE:** How value leaves the network without selling out the fuel that runs it.
**THE ONE IDEA:** Separate the instrument that brings in outside liquidity from the gas token, so raising money doesn't dilute the fuel. Built as a wrapped token plus a contracted market-maker, not the NFT bridge first imagined.
**RUNTIME:** ~4:05 · **PALETTE ACCENT:** gold · **GUIDE-MOTIF:** Pip (a single warm-amber node of light, silent, reactive)
**COLD-OPEN LINE:** "A young network needs outside money to grow. The trouble is, the only thing it has to sell is its own fuel."

> Voice: neutral warm VO; Pip reacts, never speaks. Every ON SCREEN direction is written as motion so
> an animator or video model has clear verbs. Motifs reference the Series Bible §4 dictionary; new
> motifs (wrapped shell, crossed-out bridge, stall-keeper, governance leash) are defined in the
> companion comic. Value is always light that flows, never coins. 16:9; 9:16 reflow notes where the
> composition changes.

### SCENE 1 — [0:00–0:32] — "The Only Thing to Sell Is the Fuel"  (HOOK)
MOTIF: substrate; Pip; token/SALT
ON SCREEN: black. Pip sparks into being beside a dense, bright cluster of warm-amber light, the network's fuel. It tilts outward toward the dark, wanting to reach the world. Then the fuel-cluster begins to siphon away into the dark, dimming as it goes, like a lit stage whose beams are being drained.
PIP: tilts outward, curious, then dims with unease as the fuel drains.
VO: "A young network needs outside money to grow. The trouble is, the only thing it has to sell is its own fuel, the token that pays for every bit of work the machine does. Selling that fuel to raise cash is like mortgaging your own stage. The lights dim as fast as you sell the seats. Speculators arrive, then leave the moment you need them most. The network's foundation gets weaker before it has even proven itself."
SFX/MUSIC: low warm drone; a faint draining hush as the light siphons away.
SHOT NOTES: slow push-in on the draining cluster. (9:16: cluster drains downward out of frame.)

### SCENE 2 — [0:32–1:12] — "Split the Two Jobs"  (THE IDEA)
MOTIF: token/SALT; the turn
ON SCREEN: a single gold mote strains, tugged in two directions at once, run the machine, and go raise money, flickering under the strain. Then it resolves: the fuel-light settles home, bright and stable in the lattice, while a separate carrier of light forms and steps toward the dark edge. Two clean roles. Pip surges bright.
PIP: surges bright, spins once, the "understanding" pulse.
VO: "So here's the idea. Don't make the fuel do two jobs. Let the fuel stay home and keep the machine running. Use a separate instrument, a different carrier, to take value out to the world. Raising money and running the network stop fighting over the same token. The tank stops draining."
SFX/MUSIC: the strain-flicker releases into a rising, settling swell.
SHOT NOTES: the split is a hero beat, the strained mote resolving into two calm roles. (9:16: split reads top vs bottom.)

### SCENE 3 — [1:12–1:48] — "The Bridge We Drew and Never Built"  (HOW IT WORKS 1 — honesty)
MOTIF: crossed-out bridge; slashing/danger (red); honesty beat
ON SCREEN: a ghostly blueprint span sketches itself across a dark gap in thin gold lines, detailed but clearly a plan, not a real structure. Then it dims; a stroke crosses it out; thin sting-red flickers along it. A small "?" of light hovers. Pip pulls back from the red and dims.
PIP: pulls back warily, then dims, the honest quiet beat.
VO: "Our first plan was clever, a fancy bridge that raised money as it was built. We drew it in full detail. We want to be plain about this: we never built it. Bridges hold huge pools of value, which makes them the most attacked thing in this whole space. For a small team, that's a very good reason to stop and choose something simpler."
SFX/MUSIC: music thins; a soft warning shimmer on the red, then quiet.
SHOT NOTES: keep the span obviously a drawing, never a real bridge; the cross-out must feel deliberate, not destroyed.

### SCENE 4 — [1:48–2:24] — "The Coat-Check Token"  (HOW IT WORKS 2)
MOTIF: wrapped shell; routing/travel
ON SCREEN: a mote of fuel-light is gently enclosed in a thin, clear protective shell, a lightweight claim-tag. It then glides along a lattice edge on a signed ribbon of cyan light, and a second node relays it onward, while the origin node spends nothing. Pip inspects the shell, approving.
PIP: inspects the shell, brightens, follows the glide.
VO: "What we built instead is simpler. A wrapped version of the token, like a coat-check tag. You hand over the coat, and you carry a light little tag instead. The tag moves easily while the real fuel stays safe behind the counter. And here's the kind part: you don't need gas in your pocket to send it. You just sign a slip, and someone else carries the tag on your behalf. It's a plain, auditable design, the same pattern trusted stablecoins already use every day, so it's well understood and easier to check."
SFX/MUSIC: a light, clean 'wrap' chime; effortless glide.
SHOT NOTES: the shell reads as protective and clear, never a cage. (9:16: shell center, ribbon exits upward.)

### SCENE 5 — [2:24–3:00] — "Hiring the Stall-Keeper"  (HOW IT WORKS 3)
MOTIF: stall-keeper (market-maker); token/SALT
ON SCREEN: at a busy crossing of the lattice, a single steady node tends two pans of light, adding and removing motes to keep them level, holding a fair price so value can change hands. The treasury feeds it a measured stream of light at a set rate.
PIP: watches the balancing, settles into rhythm with it.
VO: "For value to actually change hands, someone has to stand ready to buy and sell at all hours. So the network hires a market stall-keeper, a professional whose whole job is to keep prices fair and trading smooth, so a buyer always finds a seller. The treasury funds that role at a set, agreed rate, and no more. This is what seeds a real, working market, without ever selling the fuel to do it. The liquidity comes from a measured allocation, not from crowding investors onto a risky bridge."
SFX/MUSIC: steady, warm, a gentle metronome of balance.
SHOT NOTES: the two pans leveling is the readable image of 'fair price'; keep it calm and professional.

### SCENE 6 — [3:00–3:36] — "The Honest Catch"  (THE CATCH)
MOTIF: governance leash; honesty beat; token/SALT (light, never coins)
ON SCREEN: the stall-keeper node is revealed to be held by a single thin cyan tether running back to the network's ring, a leash that can be re-tied or cut. The scene cools; Pip dims; a "?" hovers. Two faint light-plates flicker: a doorway not yet open, and a plate that is deliberately not a cash promise. Value stays pure light throughout.
PIP: dims, sincere, the series' signature beat.
VO: "Now the honest part. That stall-keeper is someone we trust. The only real safety is the network's leash, its power to replace them or change their limits, and that leash is only as strong as the rules behind it. And two promises we do not make. There are no day-one cash earnings here. Being listed on a licensed exchange is still pending. What flows in this story is light, not a paycheck."
SFX/MUSIC: music thins to a single sustained note; space to breathe.
SHOT NOTES: lower brightness overall; sincere, not scary. Never render coins or dollar signs.

### SCENE 7 — [3:36–4:08] — "Why It Matters, and What's Next"  (WHY + TIE + BUTTON)
MOTIF: token/SALT; wrapped shell; series tie; Pip travels
ON SCREEN: pull back, the fuel-core stays bright and full at home while a steady ribbon of shell-wrapped light flows out to the dark edge and the balanced market glows at the crossing. Then Pip zips down an edge toward faint regions ahead, a cluster of many dots feeding one lattice, light flowing back to each.
PIP: settles, glows steadily, then darts off toward Paper VII.
VO: "Put it together, and the tank stays full while value still reaches the world. You raise money without selling out the fuel that runs everything. It's narrower than the bridge we first drew, and that's exactly why we trust it. Which leaves one last question. All this value, who should own it? Next, we pay and give ownership by contribution, not by capital."
SFX/MUSIC: full warm resolve; a single bright rising note as Pip departs.
SHOT NOTES: end card is light-only: the full core, the steady outbound ribbon. Title fades: "The Gradient Papers · No. VI". (9:16: Pip exits upward.)

## Producer notes
- **Total VO:** ~505 words ≈ 4:00–4:10 at a warm ~125 wpm. Trim Scene 4 or 5 by one sentence for a tight 3:40.
- **Hero beats:** Scene 2 (the split) and Scene 4 (the coat-check wrap + gasless glide) are the two moments to spend animation budget on.
- **Honesty beat is non-negotiable** (Scene 3 the un-built bridge, and Scene 6 the trust dependency + compliance). This paper changed more than any other in the series; the honest telling *is* the story. Never cut either to save time.
- **Music:** one evolving warm theme; reuse the series sound stamps, the 'wrap' chime (S4) is this paper's new signature, alongside the established rising departure note (S7).
- **Fact anchors:** the crossed-out bridge = the never-deployed $SNAP ERC-6551 NFT design (Paper VI §2); the coat-check token = WrappedSALT with EIP-3009 gasless authorization (§3.1); the stall-keeper = the contracted, governance-bounded MarketMakerAllocation (§3.2, §4); the compliance lines (no day-one cash, licensed-exchange pending) = §6. Do not introduce figures the paper doesn't support, and never render value as coins.
