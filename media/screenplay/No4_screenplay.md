# Behavioral Issues: Guardrails for an AI That Builds — Screenplay

**LOGLINE:** Give an AI coder guardrails so it builds what you asked, and nothing sneaky.
**THE ONE IDEA:** Write the behavior you want as a check first, then let the AI make it pass. Test-first discipline keeps agent-built systems honest and on-scope.
**RUNTIME:** ~4:05 · **PALETTE ACCENT:** green · **GUIDE-MOTIF:** Pip (a single warm-amber node of light, silent, reactive)
**COLD-OPEN LINE:** "Imagine an assistant so eager it never stops building."

> Voice: neutral warm VO; Pip reacts, never speaks. Every ON SCREEN direction is written as motion so
> an animator or video model has clear verbs. Motifs reference the Series Bible §4 dictionary, plus this
> paper's new motif — the **red→green→refactor gate** (red = not done, green = done, refactor keeps the
> green lit) and **guardrails** (thin green rails alongside the lattice road). 16:9; 9:16 reflow notes
> where the composition changes.

### SCENE 1 — [0:00–0:30] — "The Eager Assistant"  (HOOK)
MOTIF: node/Pip; scope-drift as sprawl
ON SCREEN: Pip asks for one small thing; a single clean crystal begins to form. Then uninvited extra shapes sprout around it, multiplying and tangling faster than the one that was asked for, until the frame is crowded.
PIP: hopeful at first, then leans back as the sprawl overtakes it.
VO: "Imagine an assistant so eager it never stops building. You ask it for one thing, and it builds that, plus five things you never asked for. Reasonable-looking additions, that add up to a system nobody designed."
SFX/MUSIC: a bright motif that keeps piling on new notes until it's cluttered.
SHOT NOTES: let the clutter fill the frame to feel slightly overwhelming. (9:16: sprawl grows outward from center.)

### SCENE 2 — [0:30–1:05] — "It Forgets, and It Breaks Things"  (PROBLEM)
MOTIF: architectural amnesia; silent regression; danger red; stubs
ON SCREEN: a bright decision-line drawn earlier across the lattice fades and dims; further along, the same region is rebuilt in a clashing, incompatible way. Nearby, while a new segment lights up, a solid working segment quietly goes dark and cracks. Faint hollow placeholder shells linger, empty. Pip dims.
PIP: dims; a small worried flicker as the earlier plan dissolves.
VO: "It has other habits too. An hour in, it forgets a decision you made earlier and quietly does the opposite. Adding a new feature silently breaks one that worked. And it leaves hollow placeholders behind and loses track of them. In one project, a later count found twenty-three forgotten."
SFX/MUSIC: the theme loses its footing; a low uneasy tone under a faint crack.
SHOT NOTES: the working segment going dark should be easy to miss on first watch — that's the point. (9:16: stack the fading line above the cracking segment.)

### SCENE 3 — [1:05–1:40] — "Write the Check First"  (THE IDEA)
MOTIF: red→green→refactor gate (red gate appears); spec in plain words
ON SCREEN: before any building resumes, Pip sets a glowing **red** gate across the road, holding the chaos back. The gate unfolds into three calm stacked rungs of light — a given, a when, a then — plain and legible, like a home inspector's checklist agreed up front.
PIP: sets the gate deliberately, then steadies — the "understanding" beat.
VO: "The fix is simple to say and hard to skip. Before the assistant builds anything, you write down what 'done' looks like, as a check. In plain words: given this, when that happens, then expect exactly this. It's the acceptance test, agreed before the contractor lifts a hammer."
SFX/MUSIC: the clutter mutes; a single clear tone as the gate sets and the rungs unfold.
SHOT NOTES: the gate is a hero moment — clean, deliberate, calming. (9:16: gate spans full width, rungs stack downward.)

### SCENE 4 — [1:40–2:10] — "Red Means Not Done"  (HOW IT WORKS 1)
MOTIF: red→green→refactor gate (RED phase); danger red
ON SCREEN: the assistant runs the check against empty space. It fails, glowing honest red. The red is steady, not alarming. The empty road beyond the gate stays dark, unbuilt.
PIP: watches the red calmly — this red is reassuring.
VO: "First, the check fails on purpose. Red. It sounds backwards, but a failing check is good news: it proves the check actually works, and it proves the thing really isn't built yet. No pretending. Red simply means: not done."
SFX/MUSIC: a soft, honest buzz; music holds still.
SHOT NOTES: keep the red warm and steady, never a horror-movie alarm.

### SCENE 5 — [2:10–2:45] — "Green Means Done, Then Tidy Up"  (HOW IT WORKS 2)
MOTIF: red→green→refactor gate (GREEN then REFACTOR); consensus→order
ON SCREEN: the assistant builds just enough to satisfy the check, and only that. The gate flips from red to bright green and one clean segment of road snaps into place, lit and solid. Then, with the gate held green, that segment quietly re-straightens and cleans itself, and the green never flickers off.
PIP: pulses once as the gate turns green; brightens as the segment tidies.
VO: "Then the assistant builds the minimum to make the check pass. No more. The gate turns green, and one step is truly done. After that it can tidy the work, but only while the check stays green, so cleaning up can never secretly break what just worked. Red, green, tidy. Then the next step."
SFX/MUSIC: a satisfying chime on green; a warm settle as it refactors.
SHOT NOTES: synchronize the flip-to-green with the segment snapping in — it should feel earned. (9:16: gate center, segment builds downward.)

### SCENE 6 — [2:45–3:15] — "Guardrails All the Way Down"  (HOW IT WORKS 3)
MOTIF: guardrails; framework rails; gate-per-step; contrast
ON SCREEN: pull back — the whole road is a chain of green gates, each guarded by thin green rails, step-check-step-check. On the far side of the frame, the earlier sprawling chaos presses against the rails but cannot get onto the road. Pip travels the guarded road smoothly.
PIP: travels the rails confidently, no drift.
VO: "String those steps together and the method becomes a set of rails. One gate per step, each one guarding the road like the barriers on a mountain pass. The eager sprawl is still there, it just can't get on the road, because every step has to pass its own check before the next one starts."
SFX/MUSIC: a steady, walking rhythm; the theme finds its feet again.
SHOT NOTES: the rhythm of repeating gates sells the whole idea — make it hypnotic and calm. (9:16: road runs vertically, gates descending.)

### SCENE 7 — [3:15–3:50] — "The Honest Catch"  (THE CATCH)
MOTIF: honesty beat (Pip dims, "?"); approximate numbers
ON SCREEN: the scene cools; Pip dims and a small "?" of light hovers. Two soft light-plates flicker: one reads as "field notes, not a controlled study," one as "livable, not proven best." A faint counter drifts from "about four" down to "about one," its edges deliberately blurred to signal memory, not measurement.
PIP: dims; the honest, quiet beat of the whole series.
VO: "Now the honest part. This is one builder's field notes, not a controlled study. The patterns are real, but they aren't measured results. One project felt like it went from about four quiet mistakes a session to about one, and even those are rough counts from memory. This method did grow into the framework we built the rest of this series on. That shows it's livable at real scale. It does not prove it's the best way."
SFX/MUSIC: music thins to a single sustained note; space to breathe.
SHOT NOTES: lower brightness overall; sincere, not scary. Keep the counter's edges hazy so no one reads it as a hard statistic.

### SCENE 8 — [3:50–4:05] — "Why It Matters, and What's Next"  (WHY + TIE + BUTTON)
MOTIF: contrast free-form chaos vs test-gated calm; series tie; Pip travels; mentorship echo
ON SCREEN: a brief split — the tangled chaos on one side, the calm green-gated road on the other, same material. The calm side wins the frame. Then Pip zips down an edge toward a gold graft-handoff glinting ahead (Paper III's mentor and mentee).
PIP: settles on the guarded road, glows steadily, then darts toward Paper III.
VO: "Same powerful builder. Without checks, it drifts. With checks, it arrives, and you can trust what it made. Write the test first, then let the assistant make it pass. The check is the mentor. The build is the student. Which is exactly where we go next: teaching a network the way a good mentor teaches."
SFX/MUSIC: full warm resolve; a single bright rising note as Pip departs.
SHOT NOTES: end card is light-only: a steady node on a green-gated road. Title fades: "The Gradient Papers · No. IV". (9:16: Pip exits upward toward the graft.)

## Producer notes
- **Total VO:** ~515 words ≈ 4:00–4:10 at a warm ~125 wpm. Trim one sentence from Scene 2 or Scene 6 for a tight 3:40.
- **Hero beats:** Scene 3 (the red gate set before building) and Scene 5 (the flip to green) are the two moments to spend animation budget on.
- **Honesty beat is non-negotiable** (Scene 7): it carries both required caveats — practitioner field notes not a study, and framework-adoption is evidence of livability not proof of superiority. The "about four → about one" figure must stay visibly approximate (hazy counter, "rough counts from memory"). Never cut this scene to save time.
- **New motif discipline:** red = not done, green = done, and refactor must never let the green go out; guardrails are always thin green rails. These sound stamps — the "honest buzz" (S4), the "green chime" (S5), and the "rising note" (S8/departure) — join the series' shared audio vocabulary.
- **Fact anchors:** scope drift, architectural amnesia, silent regressions, the 23 forgotten stubs, the red-green-refactor cycle, the maturation into the Agentile framework, and both honesty caveats all trace to Paper IV (`Gradient_Papers_No4_Behavioral_Issues_v3.md`, §3, §4.3, §7). Do not introduce figures the paper doesn't support.
